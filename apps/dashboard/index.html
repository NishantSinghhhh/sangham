    Why is there no QR code?

    A QR code is how a person tells their phone what to pay. Here the payer is an AI agent with no camera, so the merchant answers its request with HTTP 402 Payment Required: a machine-readable invoice (amount, token, account to pay). The agent reads it, signs a USDC transfer and retries. That 402 invoice, shown below, is the agent's QR code.

    The only QR in the picture is the merchant's existing UPI QR at their shop. The UPI ID inside it is where Sangam pays the rupees.
