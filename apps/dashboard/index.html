    What happens between USDC and UPI?Read the flow +

    The buyer signs a USDC payment to the Sangam treasury through x402. The merchant returns a fictional ticket and notifies the settlement agent. Sangam checks the Hedera record, obtains a payout-or-hold decision, and requests an INR payout from a separately funded account.

    USDC is not automatically exchanged into INR. A payout reference is not bank confirmation. HCS receipts are written only when a topic is configured. Simulation bypasses the actual purchase and chain check.
